Living Heritage Map of an Ancient Canal

A 13 km hill-fed canal — documented in Licchavi inscriptions under the name Tilmak — that once irrigated the eastern Lalitpur valley. Walked, measured, and photographed across May 2022.

What the survey data show

Heritage

🏛️
Licchavi inscriptions name the canal ‘Tilmak’ — a non-Sanskritic word that is unusual in Licchavi-era records. A pre-Licchavi (Kirat) origin is one of three working hypotheses; settling it needs further epigraphic and archaeological work.
